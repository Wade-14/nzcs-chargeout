# NZCS Forms – staff quick guide

Copy of the Claude Doc https://claude.ai/artifact/WkEqjbzb7kZtZ7N4TNMv93 (11-11-2026). When forms or app behaviour change, update this file and tell Wade the printed guide / Claude Doc may need the same change.

NZCS Forms is our app for filling in work forms on your phone. Each form becomes a PDF that is emailed to the office. It is free and comes from a web link, not the App Store or Play Store. Install it once and it sits on your home screen like any other app.

**App link:** wade-14.github.io/nzcs-chargeout

## Install on iPhone

1. Open the link in **Safari**. It must be Safari; Chrome on iPhone can't install it.
2. Tap the **Share** button (the square with an arrow pointing up) at the bottom of the screen.
3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**, then tap **Add**.
4. Open NZCS Forms from the new icon on your home screen, not from Safari.

## Install on Android

1. Open the link in **Chrome** (Samsung Internet also works).
2. If an **Install** bar appears at the top of the app, tap it and you're done.
3. If not, tap the menu (**⋮** in Chrome, **☰** in Samsung Internet), then **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**.
4. Tap **Install**, then open NZCS Forms from the new icon.

## First time you open it

1. **Enter the share code** your manager gives you. You need signal for this, once. After that the phone remembers it.
2. **The home screen shows folders:** Charge Out Forms, Job Records, Health & Safety Forms, Landowner Access Agreements, Audit Forms, and Vehicle & Equipment Checks. Tap a folder, then the form you need.
3. **Allow location and camera** when the phone asks. Location fills in addresses; the camera is for photos.
4. **To share the app** with a new staff member, tap **☰** on the home screen, then **Share the app**. It sends the link, the code and these install steps.

**Getting updates:** when the office changes a form, fully close the app (swipe it away) and open it again.

## Filling in a form

All forms work the same way: fill in top to bottom, sign, then tap **Send PDF**.

- **Required boxes:** if you miss one, Send shows it in red. Fill it in and send again.
- **Staff lists:** tap the box and tick everyone who was there. Names you tick go into the file name as initials (e.g. GC WM).
- **Location:** tap **Use my location** to fill in the address and a map. Or type the address.
- **Photos:** **Take photo** opens the camera; **Choose from gallery** picks one you already took.
- **Signatures:** pick your name, then sign in the box with your finger. Tap **Clear** to redo it.
- **Red answers on checks** (vehicle, quad, trailer, visitor, audits) mean something needs attention. It's still OK to send; the office sees them in a red box at the top of the PDF.
- **A half-filled form is kept while the app stays open.** The folder shows **Form started**. If you close the app fully, the form is wiped (Charge Out is the exception, see below).
- **Clear form and start again** wipes the form once you've sent it.

**Where forms go:** Charge Outs go to Sam. Finished incident reports go to Wade, Gareth and Toby. Every other form goes to Wade and Gareth.

## Charge Out

There are two Charge Outs in the **Charge Out Forms** folder: **Charge Out – Pest Control** and **Charge Out – Environmental Enhancement**. Pick the one for the type of work. They work exactly the same way, and each has its own Jobs in progress.

The Charge Out records each day on a job and goes to Sam once the job is finished. One job can run over several days and several phones.

1. Fill in the day: job or farm, who worked, **hours worked and travelled for each person**, KMs, materials used and comments.
2. Answer **Job complete?**
    - **No:** tap Save. The job goes into **Jobs in progress**, so anyone can add the next day from their own phone.
    - **Yes:** the job is finished. Tap **Send PDF to Sam** to send one PDF with every day.
3. Pick your name under **Filled in by** and sign.

To add a day to an existing job, open **Jobs in progress** and tap the job. To fix a past day, use its **Edit** button. Only managers can delete a job.

A Charge Out you're filling in stays on your phone even if the app is closed.

**Tracking time (Environmental Enhancement only):**

1. At the start of the day, type the job name (or open the job from Jobs in progress and tap Add day), then tap **Start tracking time now**. You can close the app.
2. At the end of the day, open **Jobs in progress → Days being tracked** on any crew phone and tap the job. Tick who worked and tap **Stop tracking time**.
3. Type any **break** in minutes and each person's **travel**. Hours worked fill in by themselves: total time (to the nearest quarter hour) minus break, minus that person's travel.
4. Finish the form and save as usual: **No** keeps the job in progress for tomorrow, **Yes** finishes it and sends it to Sam.

Start and Stop need signal.

## Incident / Accident Report

Staff fill in their part and tap **Submit report**. It isn't emailed: it goes straight to the managers, who investigate and then send the finished report. Once submitted, staff can't open it again. That keeps injury and medical details private.

**Managers** (people with the manager code) also get:

- **Incidents to investigate** on the Incident Report page, with a red badge when one is waiting. Open it, fill in the investigation, then **Save for later** or **Finish** and send.
- **Incident alerts on their phone.** Turn them on in **☰ → Incident alerts**, once per phone. On iPhone the app must be installed on the home screen first.
- **Deleting Charge Out jobs** from Jobs in progress.

## Sending: in reception vs out of reception

The app opens and forms can be filled in with no signal. **Sending, saving and submitting need signal.**

| What you're doing | In reception | Out of reception |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Opening the app, filling in forms, signing, photos | Works | Works (after you've opened the app once with signal) |
| Use my location | Address, map and GPS | GPS numbers only; type the address if you need it |
| **Send PDF** | Emails straight to the office and says **Sent to…** | Says the email didn't send. Keep the app open and tap Send again when you have signal |
| Charge Out: Save (job not complete) | Saves to Jobs in progress | Your day stays on the phone. Tap Save again in signal |
| Incident report: Submit report | Goes to the managers | Keep the app open and submit when you have signal |
| Start / Stop tracking time | Records the time | Needs signal; try again when you have it (the clock is only recorded when it reaches the office) |

**The golden rule out of reception: don't close the app** until the form is sent. Most forms are wiped when the app is fully closed. Locking the phone or switching to another app is fine.

If you can't wait for signal, tap **Share PDF instead** after a failed send. Pick your email app; it sends the email by itself once you're back in signal. Or use **Download PDF** to keep a copy on the phone.

## Rules

- **Keep the share code to NZCS staff.** Don't post it or send it outside the company.
- **Check you see "Sent to…"** before you clear a form or close the app.
- **One Charge Out per job.** Add days to the job in Jobs in progress; don't start a new one each day.
- **Fill in forms on the day**, while the details are fresh.

## If something goes wrong

| Problem | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| "Your share code has changed" | Ask your manager for the current code |
| A form you need isn't there, or looks old | Fully close the app and open it again |
| Send says the email didn't send | Get signal and tap Send again, or tap Share PDF instead |
| Use my location does nothing | Allow location for the app (or for Safari / Chrome) in your phone's settings |
| A box is red and it won't send | Fill in the red box, then tap Send again |
| The signature box won't draw | Scroll so the whole box is on screen, then sign |
| Anything else | Talk to Wade or Gareth |
